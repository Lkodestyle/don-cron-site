Don Cron

Canal de videos cortos sobre DevOps, Cloud, Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD y AI-Ops, en español rioplatense con los términos técnicos en inglés (como se habla en el trabajo real). Formato faceless, explicativo, con diagramas y animaciones, pensado para perfiles junior a semi-senior de Latinoamérica y España.

Los guiones y los videos se generan con un pipeline propio (Python, Claude API para el guion, ElevenLabs para la voz y Remotion para el render), pero cada episodio es revisado por su autor, Lucas Cristaldo, antes de publicarse. No es una cuenta automatizada sin supervisión: hay una persona real aprobando cada video.

English version

Don Cron

Don Cron is a short-video channel about DevOps, Cloud, Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD and AI-Ops, published in Spanish (Rioplatense) with English technical terms, aimed at junior-to-mid engineers in Latin America and Spain.

Scripts and videos are produced with a custom pipeline (Python, the Claude API for scripting, ElevenLabs for voice and Remotion for rendering), but every episode is reviewed by its author, Lucas Cristaldo, before it is published. It is not an unsupervised automated account — a real person approves each video.

About the Don Cron Publish app

This site also serves as the reference site for Don Cron Publish, an internal command-line tool used solely by the channel owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no third-party users and offers no login to anyone else. Full details are in the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
